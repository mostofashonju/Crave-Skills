- 10 core analytical skills: Food Costing, Contribution Margin, Menu Engineering, Break-even Analysis, Unit Economics, Pricing Strategy, Inventory and Waste Control, Sales and Customer Analytics, Delivery Platform Analytics, Cash Flow and Profitability
- Pricing anchored to dated wholesale market prices for your mentioned country and city (default: Dhaka, Bangladesh — Karwan Bazar, Chawkbazar, Town Hall, Khatunganj)
- Any recipe, beginner to advanced — Bengali and fast food — fully standardized for commercial kitchens
- 15 reusable output templates and a 14-metric KPI dashboard
