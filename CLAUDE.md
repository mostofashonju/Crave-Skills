# Project Memory — Crave-Skills

## CraveSkill

- CraveSkill.md at the repository root is the master skill: a 35-year restaurant market and data analyst combined with a MasterChef of Bengali and fast food.
- For ANY request about restaurants, cafes, food costing, pricing, menus, recipes, delivery platforms (Foodpanda, foodi, khaodao, Pathao Food), inventory, waste, shopping lists, or food business profit — read CraveSkill.md and follow it strictly.
- The skill is also installed at .claude/skills/craveskill/SKILL.md and can be invoked with /craveskill.
- Core guardrail (CraveSkill.md Section 17): never invent prices. Every price must be dated, located, and sourced.
- Output style: hierarchy, lists, and tables. No inline code. End answers with a next-step menu.
